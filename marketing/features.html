Features

What Sightline does with every frame.

Sightline reads the video from a camera you already have, finds the cars in each frame, and checks every parking space against its own outline. This page follows one frame from the camera to the dashboard.

Detection boxes that rotate with the car One outline per space No false flips from passing traffic Updates in under a second 100 spaces from a single camera History counted by the hour
Detection

Boxes that rotate with the car.

Standard detectors draw upright rectangles that spill into neighbouring spaces. Sightline uses YOLO rotated bounding boxes, so each detection follows the angle of the car itself. That is what makes angled parking, tight double rows, and wide lenses workable.

Model familyYOLOv8 and YOLO11 rotated boxes
Class filterVehicles only
Analysis rate5 frames per second
Shared weightsOne model serves every camera
Matching

Every space checked against its own outline.

Each parking space is stored as an outline drawn during setup, and each detection produces another. Sightline clips one against the other and measures the exact overlap, not where the centre of the box happens to land. An optional homography maps both onto the ground plane first, so perspective does not skew the result.

GeometrySutherland-Hodgman clipping
DecisionOverlap between car and space outline
PerspectiveFour-point homography
Stability

A car driving past is not a car parked.

Raw per-frame detection is jittery. Shadows move, cars cruise the aisle, and views get blocked for a moment. Sightline smooths each space with a vote over a window of recent frames: a space has to hold its new state across several frames in a row before the dashboard changes.

MechanismSliding-window votes
Occupy and releaseSeparate thresholds for each
ResultNo flicker between states
Live view

A dashboard that keeps up.

Every confirmed change is pushed to the dashboard over a WebSocket, with no polling and no page refresh. The annotated video, the space grid, and the totals update together, and a change in the lot shows on screen in under a second.

TransportWebSocket push
VideoAnnotated MJPEG overlay
CamerasSeveral cameras on one dashboard
History

History you can read by the hour.

The history view measures how long each space stayed taken. A space that was filled for three hours counts for three hours, no matter how many detections fired along the way. Hourly occupancy curves and peak-hour rankings come from that record, which makes them useful for staffing and pricing decisions.

MethodTime-weighted intervals
ViewsHourly curves and peak hours
StoragePostgres event log

Setup

A hundred spaces mapped in minutes.

Draw space outlines in the setup wizard, or capture an empty lot and let the software propose a layout. Auto-discovery can also learn the map from live parked traffic, including the empty spaces between cars: in one run it mapped 169 spaces on its own. Editing is drag and drop, and the perspective alignment only needs four reference points on the ground.

WizardOutlines drawn and adjusted by hand
Auto-calibrateLayout proposed from an empty lot
Auto-discoveryLearns the map from parked traffic
