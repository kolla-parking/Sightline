Product · engine + console

A live record of every space.

Sightline is one instrument with two halves. A detection engine turns the camera stream a lot already has into verified per-space occupancy. An operator console turns that occupancy into a working day. Everything on this page is shown on Lot A, the real 100-space lot we prove it on.

Live state

Every stall, resolved by name.

Sightline doesn't estimate a lot-level count — it decides each space. Oriented detection boxes rotate with every vehicle, so angled stalls and tight rows resolve to the space a car actually sits in, not the one next to it. And a space changes state only after several frames agree, so passing traffic and moving shadows never flip a reading.

History

Hours measured, not detections counted.

Every confirmed change lands in the event history with its timestamp, and analytics are computed from time occupied — a space that stays filled for three hours counts for three hours, however many detections fired along the way. The hourly curves that come out are steady enough to price and staff against, and they feed the reports you hand to an owner or a city.

Alerts

An alert is a sentence, not a firehose.

Raw detections never page anyone. Every alert Sightline raises carries a verb — what happened — and a tier — how much it matters — and repeats of the same condition collapse into one. The full stream of confirmed changes stays where it belongs: in the activity log, timestamped, ready for an audit.

Fit

Works with the cameras you have.

No poles to plant, no pucks to glue to the pavement. Sightline reads the RTSP stream nearly every IP camera already speaks — and several cameras can report into one console, so the whole property reads as a single live picture.

  • 01Any RTSP camera. No agent on the camera, no firmware to touch. When a stream drops, Sightline backs off and reconnects on its own.
  • 02Your hardware, your building. Every frame is processed on machines you control. What leaves is state — taken or open — not video. See the pipeline.
  • 03Occupancy, not surveillance. No plate reading, no person identification — the data policy says exactly what is and isn't collected.
