Anatomy of a pass

What you just watched, step by step.

The sweep above isn't an animation of an idea — it replays what the pipeline actually did with this frame. Five things happened, in order.

/01

The frame arrives

The camera's RTSP stream is decoded at five frames a second — parking happens at parking speed, and five looks a second misses nothing that matters.

rtsp · 5 fps
/02

Every vehicle is found

A YOLOv8 oriented-bounding-box model detects each car with a box that rotates to match it, so angled vehicles resolve cleanly in a crowded frame.

yolov8-obb
/03

Each detection meets the map

Every one of Lot A's 100 spaces is a polygon drawn once on the pavement. A car counts for a space only when it genuinely sits inside that outline.

100 polygons
/04

The counts settle

State holds until enough frames agree — then the tally lands. On this pass: 68 spaces taken, 32 open, 68% occupancy.

68 taken · 32 open
/05

The console hears about it

Confirmed state pushes to the operator console over WebSocket. From a change on the pavement to a change on the screen: under a second.

<1 s

Why one lot

One true thing beats ten mockups.

Sightline is pre-launch, and Lot A is not a customer network — it's one honest lot, shown honestly. We think that's worth more than a wall of invented logos, so the limits are on the record next to the results.
