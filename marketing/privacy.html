Privacy policy

effective 2026-08-03 updated 2026-08-29 version 1.1

See also The plain-language product answers — no ANPR, processing on your own hardware, state retained rather than video — live on the Data policy page. This page is the legal version.

The short version: the Sightline product is designed so that camera footage never leaves your premises, and this website collects only what you type into our forms. We do not sell anyone's data.

/01Who we are

Sightline ("we", "us") provides software that computes real-time parking occupancy from video streams. This policy covers two distinct things: (a) this marketing website, and (b) the Sightline software platform.

/02Data we collect on this website

  • Forms you submit. When you request a demo, contact us, or subscribe to our newsletter, we receive the information you enter: typically your name, email address, company, and message. We use it solely to respond to you and, for the newsletter, to send you the updates you asked for.
  • Basic technical logs. Our web server records standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and reliability. These are retained briefly and are not used for advertising.
  • No advertising trackers. This site does not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. The only thing stored in your browser is your light-or-dark theme choice.

/03Data inside the Sightline platform

Sightline is built to run on your own premises, and the design commitments below are the same ones stated in plain language in our data policy:

  • Video stays local. Camera streams are processed on hardware you control, inside your own network. Raw footage is not transmitted to us and is not required to leave your building.
  • Occupancy, not identity. The platform detects vehicles as geometric objects and computes per-space occupancy. There is no ANPR in the system: it performs no license-plate reading and no face recognition, and it cannot identify a vehicle or a person.
  • State, not video, is retained. What the platform stores is occupancy state — which spaces were taken or open, and when. History is a log of state changes, useful for planning and audits, useless for tracking people.
  • You are the controller. For any personal data that may incidentally appear in your own camera footage, you (the operator) act as data controller; Sightline software acts under your configuration and control.

/04How we use information

  • To respond to demo requests and enquiries.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in each email or by contacting us).
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

/05Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run this website and our communications (e.g., email delivery), under contractual confidentiality, or where required by law.

/06Retention

Form submissions are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain our business records. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Server logs rotate on a short schedule.

/07Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us via the contact page. We'll respond within the timeframe your local law requires.

/08Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but minimising what we collect in the first place is our primary defence.

/09Children

This website and the Sightline platform are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

/10Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the dates above. Material changes to how we handle existing data will be flagged prominently.

/11Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us through the contact page.
