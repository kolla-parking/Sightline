Questions

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

If yours isn't here, ask us directly — we answer the same way we write.

Do I need to buy new cameras?

Almost certainly not. Sightline reads standard RTSP streams — the protocol nearly every security camera made in the last decade already speaks. If your lot is on camera today, that camera is your sensor. If one corner has no coverage, any ordinary camera fills the gap; there is no proprietary hardware to buy from us at any price.

Where does our footage go?

Nowhere. Detection runs on your hardware, inside your network. The camera streams to a machine you control, and what Sightline keeps is the state of each space — not video. Footage never leaves your building on any plan. The data policy spells this out.

What are the contract terms?

Plans are monthly, and no annual lock-in is required. Because Sightline is pre-launch, final terms — including any early-operator arrangement — are agreed together on your demo call and written down before anything is charged.

What counts as a site?

One physical property: a surface lot, a garage, or a lot and garage that share an address and an operations team. All the cameras on that property count as one site. A portfolio spread across town is multiple sites — which is what the Multi-site plan is for.

Can I move between plans?

Yes. A plan is a monthly setting, not a construction project. Move up when you add cameras or sites, move down if you scale back, and the change takes effect on your next cycle.
