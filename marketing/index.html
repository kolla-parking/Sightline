Data policy

Occupancy, not surveillance.

Cities and operators have to answer for every camera. Sightline is built to make that answer easy.

  • 01No plate reading. Sightline detects whether a space is occupied — it has no ANPR and never identifies a vehicle or a person.
  • 02Footage stays in your building. Every frame is processed on your hardware. What leaves is state — taken or open — not video.
  • 03State, not video, is retained. History is a log of occupancy changes, useful for planning and audits, useless for tracking people.
