What we believe

Parking data shouldn't require tearing up asphalt. Your cameras already see every space. Sightline turns what they see into numbers you can use.

Every frame becomes a live count of which spaces are taken and which are free, computed on your own hardware. Nothing leaves your network.

Why we built it →
What it does

One camera feed in.
Live occupancy out.

Sightline takes any standard camera stream, finds the vehicles in it, and checks each one against a map of your spaces. The result is a live picture of your whole lot.

  • ✓Works with the cameras you have. If a camera can stream video, Sightline can read it.
  • ✓Counts each space, not just the lot. Every space has its own outline and its own live state.
  • ✓Ignores passing traffic. A space changes state only after several frames agree, so cars driving by don't flip it.

The same lot as Sightline sees it: 68 spaces taken, 32 open.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

1.Connect a camera

Point Sightline at your camera's stream. It handles drops and reconnects on its own.

2.Map your spaces

Draw each space once, or let Sightline propose the whole layout from a photo of the empty lot.

3.Detection runs

Each frame, every vehicle is found and matched to the space it sits in. Angled cars and tight rows are fine.

4.Watch it live

The dashboard updates the moment a space changes, with the annotated video right next to the counts.

Features

What's inside.

All features →

Angled detection

Detection boxes rotate to match each car, so angled spots and tight rows still read correctly.

Space-by-space matching

Each space is an exact outline on the pavement. A car counts only when it actually sits inside one.

Steady readings

A space flips only after several frames agree. Shadows and passing cars don't cause false changes.

Instant updates

Changes reach the dashboard the moment they happen, next to the live annotated video.

Hourly history

Busy hours are measured by how long spaces actually stay filled, so the history reflects reality.

Quick setup

Draw your spaces once, or let Sightline find them on its own from an empty lot or live traffic.
