About

The cameras were already there.

Sightline started with a simple observation: nearly every parking lot already has a camera pointed at it. The missing piece was software that reads what the camera sees.

G
Our goal

Make every parking space visible without installing new hardware and without sending footage to someone else's cloud.

The story

Why we built it.

Ground sensors cost money for every single space, and installing them means construction: closing rows, cutting pavement, running power.

Gate counters only count entries and exits. They can tell you how many cars came in, but they can never tell a driver where an open space actually is.

The camera on the pole already sees every space at once. Sightline reads that view and turns it into live occupancy, one reading per space.

One camera covering the 100-space sample lot.

Principles

How we work.

1.

Footage stays in your building.

Sightline runs on your own hardware. Video is processed where it is captured and never leaves your network. Only occupancy states, small structured records, move to the dashboard.

2.

Every reading can be explained.

Each occupancy decision traces to a measurable overlap between a detected car and a space outline. If a space changes state, we can show you exactly why.

3.

It has to run for months, not demos.

Streams reconnect on their own, no camera gets starved of processing time, and space states do not flicker. That unglamorous work is most of the product.

4.

Your hardware, your data.

No proprietary boxes and no per-space sensor bills. If a camera speaks RTSP it is compatible, and the data it produces belongs to you.

Values

Your data is yours.

Live states, dwell history, and peak curves are all yours to see and keep. Operators can always export their own data without asking us.

We count cars, not people.

There is no face recognition and no license-plate recognition. The dashboard knows a space is taken, not who took it.

We say how it works.

We explain how detection works, where it struggles, and what each threshold means, so you can check our readings against what you see on the ground.

Small footprint.

One shared model runs across every camera, so a single modest machine can cover the whole lot.
